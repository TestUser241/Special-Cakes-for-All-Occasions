Cakes for Sale

Here are some of Special Cakes for All Occasions desserts that have been loved by thousands of customers!


Brownies

A baked chocolate dessert that is usually cut into squares and can have a fudgy or cakey texture

Allergy alert: Contains dairy, nuts and eggs

Cheesecakes

A sweet dessert that features a creamy filling that is made from fresh cheese, sugar and eggs. It is usually sat on a cookie or pastry crust and is chilled

Allergy alert: Contains gluten and dairy

Macarons

A French dessert that is composed of two smooth, round domed cookies that is sandwiched together with a creamy filling

Allergy alert: Contains gluten, dairy, nuts and eggs

Cupcakes

Small, individual cakes that are usually topped with frosting, icing or other sweet decorations and are designed to serve one person, perfect for special events!

Allergy alert: Contains gluten, dairy, nuts and eggs

Speciality Cakes

Special made cakes for the most special occasions such as weddings or birthdays. Crafted with unique flavors that can be tailored to any special event, the choice is yours!

Allergy alert: Can be made to match dietary requirements

Flavoured Cakes

Baked cakes or desserts that contain special ingredients that give the batter a distinct and unique taste. Perfect for when you want a unique cake with excellent flavour!

Allergy alert: Contains gluten, dairy, nuts and eggs. Strawberry cakes contain strawberries.
